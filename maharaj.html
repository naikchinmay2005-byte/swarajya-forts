A life that shaped a legacy

From the hills of Shivneri
to the throne at Raigad.

Shivaji Maharaj was born at Shivneri Fort in 1630. In the 17th century Deccan, he built an independent kingdom through bold leadership, careful statecraft and a network of strategically held forts.

His coronation at Raigad in 1674 marked the formal establishment of Hindavi Swarajya. His story is remembered not only through battles, but through the institutions, maritime vision and ideals of self-rule he championed.
